A group is just a code. Everyone who types the same code sees each other's score for the day. No email, no password.

Group scores turn on once the game is hosted. For now you will only see your own.

How to play

  1. Five stops a day, one try. Everyone gets the same five, and a new trip arrives at midnight. After that, bonus trips are just for fun.
  2. Spin and zoom the globe, tap where you think the place is, then lock in your guess.
  3. The closer you are, the more points. A perfect 100 means your pin is right on the spot, and it is rare.
  4. Score 70 or better and that country stamps your passport for good.
  5. The last stop is a picture. No name, just a photo. Work out where in the world it was taken.
  6. Learn something at every stop, then share your page with your family or class.

Explorer gives a hint at each stop and scores gently. Navigator gives no hints, and the harder stops are worth double and triple.
